For Graduate Students

Current UNM Political Science graduate students interested in affiliating with the Institute for Study of Rights and Repression should contact either director. We welcome students whose research engages with voting rights, redistricting, immigration policy, human rights, state repression, or computational/statistical methods for political analysis.
